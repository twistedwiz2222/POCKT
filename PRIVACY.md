# POCKT privacy promise

- Financial records stay on the device.
- POCKT has no `INTERNET` permission.
- The app stores amount, merchant, category, time, source app, transaction direction, and a one-way duplicate fingerprint.
- Raw notification text is not stored.
- POCKT never reads payment screens, PINs, OTPs, SMS messages, contacts, or location.
- Users can remove individual entries or delete all POCKT data from Settings.

Before public distribution, add an in-app disclosure immediately before notification access, publish a matching privacy policy, test every supported notification format, and complete Google Play's Data safety declarations.
